# Maximum Numbers of Real Equiangular Lines in Selected Dimensions

Valjakas Djaljapayan, Zhen-Qi Liao, and Wei-Hsuan Yu

Department of Mathematics, National Central University

The authors collaborated with OpenAI’s GPT-6 on the research presented in these manuscripts.

This repository collects manuscripts, exact certificates, and verification programs for the maximum numbers of real equiangular lines in dimensions 18, 19, and 20.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Manuscript and verification materials |
| --- | --- |
| [Dimension 18](dimension-18/) | Computer-assisted proof of $N(18)=57$, with exact certificates and replay instructions. |
| [Dimension 19](dimension-19/) | Computer-assisted proof of $N(19)=72$, with a verified 72-line construction, exact certificates, and replay instructions. |
| [Dimension 20](dimension-20/) | Computer-assisted proof of $N(20)=90$, with exact verification programs and replay instructions. |

Each supplement lists its dependencies, verification commands, and expected outputs.

For links in earlier copies of the dimension 18 manuscript, see the [current paper and verification materials](dimension-18/).

## Combined manuscript

The [combined verification guide](combined-reproduction/) covers the three dimensional supplements, the nested Leech sections, and the sharp fixed-angle examples. The combined manuscript and its LaTeX source are maintained separately.
