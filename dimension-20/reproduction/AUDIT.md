# Proof dependencies and verification scope

The manuscript reduces a hypothetical 91-line system to common cosine $1/5$ and embeds the associated rank-21 lattice in a positive definite even unimodular lattice of rank 24. The reduction uses the fixed-angle bounds proved in the article, Meyer's theorem, and the Niemeier classification.

For each general rooted model, the scalar verifier checks the supplied glue subgroup for closure, order, even minimum squares, and minimum square at least four outside the zero class. The subgroups can also be regenerated by the [subgroup search used in dimension 19](../../dimension-19/reproduction/audit/niemeier20_independent.py) with the dimension-twenty Cartan-coordinate data. The replay uses the supplied subgroups as inputs. It checks the inequalities on local orbit signatures and covers all scalar profiles and their local alternatives. The two small models use the explicit generator or kernel descriptions and verify the signed permutations used to group their profiles. Their final bounds can incorporate the dimension-twenty projection constraints stated in the manuscript.

For the rooted binary model, the computation verifies the code and the partition bounds used in the analysis of the marking cases. The Leech-lattice argument uses the classical coordinate description and transitivity on norm-six vectors. The incidence checker recomputes the short table, minimum-lift cases, and the 90-line incidence count from exact small integer and rational calculations. The upper-bound proof uses the manuscript's argument about the evaluation code and its subspaces. The replay's incidence check does not enumerate the full evaluation code.

The reference transcripts are those supplied with the manuscript. Reproducing them checks the stated finite calculations. The mathematical reductions, including the frame bounds, the analytic Golay and Leech arguments, and the embedding into rank 24, remain in the manuscript.
